QUICK START

Up and running in 30 seconds.

No setup, no sign-in, no clutter. Add your first student, hand out a few points, and you'll get the hang of it instantly.

01

Add, award & manage

  1. 1

    Award points with the + button.

    Pick a quick award of +10, +7, +15, or +20 — or type any custom positive amount. Scores update live.

  2. 2

    Students rename themselves.

    Names here are read-only — students can update their own username from their own page, with their PIN. Use the trash icon to remove a student permanently.

  3. 3

    Tap the clock icon to see history.

    Every award is logged with the time it happened. Open the history dialog to see them grouped by day, with a per-day total.

02

Member status at a glance

Every student gets a live status based on their activity. Tap the Member Status card in the dashboard to see them sorted.

Active

Awarded points in the last 24 hours and holds a positive score. Keep the momentum going.

Ghost

No points awarded in 24 hours, or sitting at zero. A quick award brings them back to active.

03

Keyboard shortcuts & quick tips

Esc

Close any open dialog.

Enter

Submit the Custom Award field.

Type

Start typing in the search bar to filter students live.

Click

Copy in the top bar grabs a leaderboard-formatted list.

04

Share content with the + button

The floating + button in the bottom-right corner opens a popup with four options. Each one posts to the active list on every student's page.

New Challenge

A prompt with a custom expiry you pick (1 minute to 30 days out).

Daily Challenge

A 24-hour prompt that auto-expires at midnight tomorrow.

Notification

A one-way message that auto-archives as soon as every student has viewed it.

Message

Like a notification, but students can reply with text and an audio recording. Stays active until you archive it.

For challenges, pick a return type in the dialog: Text (a written reflection), Image (students upload a photo), Video (a short clip), or Audio (a recording). The student's card on their page shows a Submit button scoped to that type. Every reply has BOTH a text part and a media part, so a student can write a note with their image, or just send a recording with no text — fill one, fill both, or fill neither (the form requires at least one).

05

Read student replies

In the Gradebook section, every student row has a Replies button in the Actions column (a speech-bubble icon). Tap it to open a popup showing every reply that student has made to a challenge or message, newest first. Each reply is labeled with which message or challenge it was for, and shows both the text and the media (image, video, or audio) inline.

Students can post more than one reply to the same challenge or message — useful for follow-ups, edits, or audio updates. The teacher-side gradebook is the canonical record of awards; replies are conversation, not points.
