    QUICK START

    Up and running in 30 seconds.

    No setup, no sign-in, no clutter. Add your first student, hand out a few points, and you'll get the hang of it instantly.

    01

    Add, award & manage

    1. 1

      Award points with the + button.

      Pick a quick award of +10, +7, +15, or +20 — or type any custom positive amount. Scores update live.

    2. 2

      Double-click a name to rename.

      No edit button needed — just double-click any student's name to rename them. Use the trash icon to remove a student permanently.

    3. 3

      Tap the clock icon to see history.

      Every award is logged with the time it happened. Open the history dialog to see them grouped by day, with a per-day total.

    02

    Member status at a glance

    Every student gets a live status based on their activity. Tap the Member Status card in the dashboard to see them sorted.

    Active

    Awarded points in the last 24 hours and holds a positive score. Keep the momentum going.

    Ghost

    No points awarded in 24 hours, or sitting at zero. A quick award brings them back to active.

    03

    Keyboard shortcuts & quick tips

    Esc

    Close any open dialog.

    Enter

    Submit the Custom Award field.

    Type

    Start typing in the search bar to filter students live.

    Click

    Copy in the top bar grabs a leaderboard-formatted list.
